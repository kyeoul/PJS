Instructions:

Welcome to PJs! We bring you a competitive activity inspired by 20 Questions that incorporates strategy, suspense, and thrilling gameplay. By allowing up 
to four players to play at once against each other, trying to guess one another's identities, we've created a game that takes the classic to new heights. 
The possibilities are endless! Have fun!

Set-up: 

Join a call with your friends. Have one person set up a room on PJs and let everyone join. That's it!


Gameplay:

One person asks a YES/NO question to another member in the party. The question's recipient answers the question and proceeds to ask a question themselves. This chain of
questioning sets the stage for the game. Whenever any player wants, they can guess another player's identity in the PJs interface. They will be informed of the accuracy of
their guess. However, no one else will know the results of the guess (or the fact that someone guessed in the first place). The game continues until one player whose
identity has not been discovered remains. All players can ask questions throughout the entirety of the game (even if their identity has already been guessed). The aim
of the game is to keep track of who you think has been eliminated and who you think still needs to be discovered! 


Rules:

1) All questions must be answered honestly. If one does not know the answer to a question, the questioner
is permitted to ask another question.
2) No one can disclose the state of another player (whether or not another's identity has been guessed)
3) Do not over-guess. This can be talked about prior to the game.
4) Allying is not permitted. All communication must take place in one call.
5) Each identity must be a person known by all members of the party.
6) One cannot use the internet.
7) One cannot keep a log of questions asked.
